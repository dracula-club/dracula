WHERE PASSION MEETS THE PROSCENIUM

Two Decades of Raw Theatrical Fire & Cultural Mastery

Founded in 2003, DRACULA is the official Dramatic & Cultural Association of Shri Ramswaroop Memorial College of Engineering & Management (SRMCEM), Lucknow. What began as a collective of passionate student storytellers has evolved into one of Northern India's most decorated collegiate drama societies.

From high-octane street theatre circles (नुक्कड़ नाटक) tackling burning social realities to grand proscenium auditorium productions with live acoustic percussion and celestial lighting choreographies, DRACULA embodies the raw truth that theatre is not just an art — it is the mirror of life.

Stage Play • Proscenium Production

Yamraj Ki Adalat

🏆 Best Presentation Award — Abhivyakti 2K26

A sharp-witted satirical courtroom drama questioning mortal hypocrisy, bureaucratic complacency, and moral responsibility in the celestial court of Yamraj. Showcases the collaborative power of writing, acting, and lighting design.

Writer: Saurabh Mishra
Director: Saurabh Mishra
Accolade: Best Presentation Award, Abhivyakti 2K26
Nukkad Natak • नुक्कड़ नाटक

Samaj (समाज)

A thunderous street-theatre campaign confronting social divides, collective apathy, and modern double standards through sharp satirical verse and acoustic chorus.

Director: Anshit Giri
Writer: Prabuddha Singh
Classical Stage Play

Main Toh Girdhar Ki (मैँ तो गिरधर की)

A poetic proscenium drama portraying the divine defiance, devotional surrender, and spiritual journey of Meerabai with classical set and proscenium lighting choreography.

Writer: Anshika
