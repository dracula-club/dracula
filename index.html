WHAT IS DRACULA?

Not an ordinary college club. A 20-year dramatic society built on raw artistic hunger, proscenium stagecraft, and the undisputed power of student expression.

20+ YEARS ON STAGE

“When the auditorium lights dim and the spotlight strikes, pretenses vanish. Only pure truth remains.”

2003 Founded
5× Champions
100% Live Stage

As the Dramatic & Cultural Association of SRMCEM, DRACULA is the crucible of student actors, playwrights, dholak percussionists, and backstage architects who command auditoriums and street circles alike.

THE FOUR PILLARS OF DRAMATIC EXPRESSION
WING 01 • PROSCENIUM 🎭

Proscenium Stage Plays

Grand auditorium productions commanding intense multi-character arcs, celestial courtrooms, classical dialogues, and proscenium lighting choreography.

Flagship: Yamraj Ki Adalat (Abhivyakti 2K26 Winner)
WING 02 • STREET THEATRE 📢

Nukkad Natak • नुक्कड़ नाटक

High-decibel, unapologetic street circles armed with roaring vocal choruses, thundering live dholak percussion, and satirical verse confronting social apathy.

Flagship: Samaj & TRP Ka Circus
WING 03 • SOLO SPOTLIGHT 🕯️

Monoacts & Monologues

Intimate psychological studies stripped down to a solitary proscenium beam. Demanding micro-expression mastery, psychological depth, and vocal control.

Flagship: Alone Spotlight Solo Showcase
WING 04 • LIVE ENSEMBLE 🥁

Live Percussion & Stagecraft

Acoustic live dholak, harmonium, flute, scriptwriting, lighting coordination, and stage management driving the rhythmic heartbeat of every performance.

Ensemble: 40+ Actors, Musicians & Crew

Stage Play • Proscenium Production

Yamraj Ki Adalat

🏆 Best Presentation Award — Abhivyakti 2K26

A sharp-witted satirical courtroom drama questioning mortal hypocrisy, bureaucratic complacency, and moral responsibility in the celestial court of Yamraj. Showcases the collaborative power of writing, acting, and lighting design.

Writer: Saurabh Mishra
Director: Saurabh Mishra
Accolade: Best Presentation Award, Abhivyakti 2K26
Nukkad Natak • नुक्कड़ नाटक

Samaj (समाज)

A thunderous street-theatre campaign confronting social divides, collective apathy, and modern double standards through sharp satirical verse and acoustic chorus.

Director: Anshit Giri
Writer: Prabuddha Singh
Nukkad Natak • National Finalist

TRP Ka Circus

An electrifying street play exposing the sensationalism, fabricated hysteria, and ratings hunger of modern media circuses.

Director: Aditya Singh
Recognition: 🏆 Finalist — IIT Roorkee
Classical Stage Play

Main Toh Girdhar Ki (मैं तो गिरधर की)

A poetic proscenium drama portraying the divine defiance, devotional surrender, and spiritual journey of Meerabai.

Writer: Anshika
Director: Honey Singh
Mythological Proscenium

Sita Swayamvar (सीता स्वयंवर)

Recreating the royal assembly of Mithila, the pride of monarchs, and Lord Rama lifting the celestial Shiva Dhanush.

Format: Multi-Character Mythological Staging
Scale: Full Ensemble & Classical Costumes
Solo Monoact • Monologue

Alone Spotlight

An intimate psychological study of human isolation, emotional vulnerability, and courage under a single dramatic spotlight.

Format: Solo Dramatic Performance
Discipline: Micro-Expressions & Voice Control
Grand Stage Production

Mahabharat (महाभारत)

A monumental proscenium production capturing the cosmic clash of Dharma, moral dilemmas, and destiny on the Kurukshetra battlefield.

Scale: Grand Ensemble Production
Direction: DRACULA team
