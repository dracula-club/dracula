Clear Your Doubts

Frequently Asked Questions

I have zero theatre or acting experience. Can I still audition?
Absolutely yes! More than 70% of Dracula's most decorated actors, including former Secretaries, had never set foot on a proscenium stage before joining the club. We look for raw hunger, expressiveness, dedication, and the courage to stand in the circle. All acting, voice modulation, and stage presence skills are taught during our training bootcamps.
Do I need to prepare a monologue or performance in advance?
If you have a prepared Hindi or English monologue, poetry recitation, or character scene, we'd love to see it! But if you don't have one prepared, that is completely fine — our judges will provide on-the-spot improv prompts, emotional situations, or short dialogues for you to perform.
Can 1st year B.Tech / MCA / MBA students audition?
Yes! Freshers are the lifeblood of DRACULA. Every year, a massive portion of our major collegiate productions (both in Nukkad Natak and Stage Plays) are cast with brilliant 1st year students. Joining in 1st year allows you to grow into senior leadership roles.
I don't want to act. Can I join for Filmmaking, Dholak, or Technical Crew?
Yes! DRACULA has dedicated non-acting wings: Cinema & Filmmaking (Camera, Editing, Script), Music & Rhythm (Live Dholak, Harmonium, Vocals), and Stagecraft (DMX Lights, Sound Mixing, Makeup, Set Design). Simply select your preferred wing on the form above.
Will rehearsals interfere with my engineering studies and exams?
Not at all. We are all SRMCEM engineering students ourselves. Rehearsals are scheduled after class hours (from 5:30 PM onwards), and during mid-semesters or university semester exams, all club activities are paused so everyone can study. Dracula seniors also provide academic mentorship and study materials to freshers!
