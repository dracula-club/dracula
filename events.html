No prior theatre or stagecraft experience required. Whether you hunger to command proscenium stage lights, roar in a high-octane street theatre circle, play live dholak percussion, choreograph lighting, or craft original scripts — DRACULA is your creative crucible at SRMCEM.

🎭
Stage Actors
Classical proscenium plays, multi-character arcs, dialogue delivery & voice control.
📢
Street Play Ensemble
High-energy Nukkad Natak, social satire, rhythmic movements & courtyard circles.
🥁
Live Percussion & Chorus
Dholak, duff, harmonium, vocal chorus chanting & authentic live music accompaniment.
💡
Stagecraft & Script
Proscenium lighting choreo, sound coordination, scriptwriting & backstage architecture.
